# Relation to DIF and DRP

DI is not a replacement for DIF or DRP.

It is the middle layer between intent clarification and decision commitment.

## Layer Roles

```text
DIF → clarify intent
DI  → clarify limits
DRP → commit decision
```

## DIF

DIF asks:

> What does the human actually mean?

DIF transforms raw signals into clarified intent.

## DI

DI asks:

> What can and cannot be done under the current constraints?

DI maps capabilities, limitations, risks, unknowns, and feasible paths.

## DRP

DRP asks:

> What decision was made, why, by whom, and with what causal links?

DRP records committed decisions.

## Combined Principle

```text
DIF — do not act until intent is understood.
DI  — do not promise action until limits are understood.
DRP — do not forget why a decision was made.
```

## System Flow

```text
Raw signal
→ Intent clarification
→ Limitation mapping
→ Decision commitment
→ Decision record
```

## Boundary

DI ends when a decision is ready to be recorded.

DRP begins when a decision becomes a commitment.
