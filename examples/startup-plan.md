# Example: Startup Plan

## Request

> Build a startup plan that can reach $100k/month revenue.

## Inferred Intent

The user wants a realistic path from idea to monetizable product, with constraints and feasible next actions made explicit.

## Capabilities

- clarify target customers,
- map business assumptions,
- identify revenue models,
- define validation steps,
- produce a staged execution plan.

## Limitations

- market demand is not yet validated,
- founder availability may be constrained,
- budget and runway may be unknown,
- legal, tax, and jurisdictional requirements may be missing,
- revenue cannot be guaranteed.

## Constraints

- time constraint,
- capital constraint,
- customer discovery requirement,
- market validation requirement,
- compliance boundary.

## Feasible Actions

- define a narrow customer segment,
- list falsifiable market assumptions,
- create a 30-day validation plan,
- identify 3-5 paid pilot candidates,
- define measurable traction criteria.

## Blocked Actions

- promising revenue without validation,
- scaling before confirming demand,
- treating an idea as a business model without customers,
- committing to hiring or spend without runway analysis.

## Recommended Next Step

Convert the startup goal into a feasibility map: customer, problem, willingness to pay, distribution channel, constraints, and first validation action.
